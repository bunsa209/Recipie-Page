        <h2>Simple Chocolate cake</h2>
        <p>This is a very basic recipie of a Chocolate cake if you are craving for one but does not aim for anything fancy!</p>

        <h3>Ingredients</h3>
        <ol>
            <li>All-purpose flour - 1 cup</li>
            <li>Sugar - 1 cup</li>
            <li>Cocoa Powder - 1/4 cup</li>
            <li>Baking soda - 1/4 teaspoon</li>
            <li>baking powder - 1/2 teaspoon</li>
            <li>salt - 1/4 teaspoon</li>
            <li>Coffee powder - 1/2 teaspoon</li>
            <li>Butter Milk - 1/2 cup</li>
            <li>Oil - 1/2 cup</li>
            <li>Hot Milk - 1/2 cup</li>
            <li>Egg - 1</li>
        </ol>
        <h3>Alternates for ingredients</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Vanilla Essence instead of Coffee Powder</li>
            <p>Coffee Powder definetly brings out the flavour of Chocolate cakes in a dense way but if you are a fan of vanilla flavour with Chocolate then you can give this a try , make sure to add just a small amount.</p>
            <li>Almond flour or any other gluten free flour instead of All-purpose</li>
            <p>If you are looking for some healthy diet options</p>
        </ul>
        <h3>Method of Cooking</h3>
        <ul>
            <li>Ingredients Preparation Time: 10 minutes</li>
            <li>Bake Time: 35 minutes</li>
        </ul>
        <ol>
            <li>Preheat Oven to 350F. </li>
            <li>Mix together all dry ingredients.</li>
            <li>In other bowl, mix in all of wet ingredients.</li>
            <li>Combine the dry and wet ingredients together.</li>
            <li>Line a cake baking tin with parchment paper or grease with Butter or oil. </li>
            <li>Bake for 30-40 minutes depending on the size of your cake tin. Make sure not to burn it. Keep an eye on the oven while baking.</li>
            <li>After 30 mins, instert a fork or toothpick at the centre to see if it comes out clean. If it is clean it means cake is cooked , if not keep it for 5 or 10 mins more.</li>
            <li>Cool for some time and enjoy with some hot coffee , whipping cream or any sweet sauces of youur choice.</li>
        </ol>
